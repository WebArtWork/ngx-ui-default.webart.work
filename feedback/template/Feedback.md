# Feedback

Write freely, in your own words — there's no wrong way to do this. If a section doesn't apply to you, just delete it.

## 👋 Overall impression

How did the software feel? What was your first impression?

## ❤️ What I liked

What worked well? What felt useful, easy, fast, or well designed?

## 😕 What confused me

Was anything difficult to understand? Did you ever not know what to do next?

## 👎 What I didn't like

Anything annoying, unnecessary, difficult, slow, or poorly designed?

## 💡 Ideas & suggestions

What would you change or improve? Any ideas that could make the software better?

## 🧩 What was missing

Did you expect something that wasn't there? Are there features you would want?

## 🤔 Would I use this?

Yes / Maybe / No

Why?

## 💬 Anything else

Anything else you want to tell us — write freely.
