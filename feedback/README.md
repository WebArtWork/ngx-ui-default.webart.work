# Feedback

This folder is where we collect honest, real feedback from people who try out the software — no technical knowledge required.

You don't need to know how to code, how to file a bug report, or how to use Git beyond the basics below. Just use the software, write down what you noticed, and share it.

## How it works

1. **Fork this repository.**
2. **Clone your fork** to your computer.
3. **Open the `feedback` folder as an Obsidian vault.** (This makes it easy to write notes and see embedded screenshots. [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) is free.)
4. **Copy the entire `feedback/template` folder** into `feedback/reviews`.
5. **Rename your copied folder using your full name.**
   Example:
   `feedback/reviews/John Smith/`
6. **Fill in `Feedback.md`** with your experience, opinions, ideas, and suggestions.
7. **Fill in `Bugs.md`** with anything that didn't work correctly.
8. **Add any relevant screenshots** to your folder's `attachments/` subfolder.
9. **Embed your screenshots** in the Markdown files where they're relevant (the templates show you how).
10. **Remove the example content and `sample.png`** once you're done — those are just there to show you the format.
11. **Commit your feedback** to your fork.
12. **Open a Pull Request** back to this repository.

That's it — your feedback becomes a folder with your name on it, ready for us to read.

## What we're looking for

Write naturally, like you're telling a friend about the software. We want your honest reactions:

- Things you liked
- Things you disliked
- Things that confused you
- Things you expected to exist but didn't find
- Ideas you had while using it
- Anything that just felt wrong or off

## What you don't need to do

- You don't need any technical knowledge.
- You don't need to fill in every section — if a section doesn't apply to you, delete it.
- You don't need to classify, prioritize, rate severity, assign IDs, or write anything like a formal bug ticket.

Just describe what happened, in your own words. AI agents will handle turning feedback into structured, actionable work later — that part isn't your job.

## Questions?

If anything about this process is unclear, that's useful feedback too — write it down in your `Feedback.md`.
